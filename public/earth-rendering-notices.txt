World Echo Earth renderer
WebGL adaptation of Three.js r186 examples/webgpu_tsl_earth.html, based on Bruno Simon / Three.js Journey Earth lesson.
https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/r186/examples/webgpu_tsl_earth.html
Changes: adapted to WebGL MeshStandardMaterial, shared scene lighting, independent moving cloud layer, procedural ocean normals, display-only relief and the World Echo coordinate system.
